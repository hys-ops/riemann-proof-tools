Recover earlier diagrams from the final row

Separate mechanism · Local formal checks

Read the standalone proof (PDF)

Input. A weakly decreasing integral weight row Λ from the constructed family and an earlier cutoff Nᵢ with an available boundary value Λ(Nᵢ). The correspondence to the family is an input.

Output. Earlier row lengths, cell membership, and area from one truncation and one subtraction per row, without a recursive traversal of the earlier shapes.

Proof

Index rows from zero. Retain Λ(0),…,Λ(Nᵢ−1) and subtract the common boundary value Λ(Nᵢ). The resulting sequence Lᵢ(r)=Λ(r)−Λ(Nᵢ) is nonnegative integral and weakly decreasing, so it defines a Young diagram. Its cells are precisely pairs (r,c) with 0≤r<Nᵢ and 0≤c<Lᵢ(r), and its area is Σ_{r<Nᵢ}Lᵢ(r). Once the constructed family’s row-length identity is known, these elementary deductions determine all earlier diagrams from the final row.

Why the identity holds in this family

Write h=m−1 for the final stage and Nᵢ=k+i(t+1). The row formulas have a head of length k followed by blocks of length t+1. Write eᵢ for the cumulative input to stage i. In the head (r<k), final length minus stage-i length is e_h−eᵢ−(h−i)(t+1), because the length formulas differ only in e and N. Within an earlier block q<i the formula differs by that same constant: the block’s own parameters cancel. At the first row of block i, r=Nᵢ, the final formula evaluates to exactly that constant if i<h. At i=h, pad the row by zero at N_h. Thus the common offset is Λ(Nᵢ) throughout the retained rows.

Application

The manuscript family identifies its earlier row lengths with this expression at Nᵢ=k+i(t+1). This is a discrete structural identity, not a use of tH′−H. Reusing the final row avoids rebuilding whole intermediate diagram checks. In the pinned comparison, declarations through nuRowOK went from 203 lines to 80; the net patch after preserving common lemmas and other callers saved 32 lines. These are different measurements, not additive.

Verification boundary

The local Lean declarations rowLen_final and nuRow_final check the reconstruction in the pinned setting. Project inputs and number-field assumptions remain explicit. This note is not a proof of the complete construction or manuscript.
